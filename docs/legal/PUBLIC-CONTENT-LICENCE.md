## Practical steps for the releases repository

1. Put a `LICENSE-docs.txt` holding the CC BY-ND 4.0 legal code, and this notice in its README.
2. Put the EULA and the NOTICE files beside every installer.
3. Do not publish anything from `docs/plan/`, `Manuals/` or the source code.
