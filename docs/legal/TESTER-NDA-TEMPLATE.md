# Alpha tester confidentiality agreement (template)

> **DRAFT template. Have a Ugandan advocate review it before use.** Fill in the bracketed items.

This agreement is between **AfroBIM** [registered name, address] ("AfroBIM") and **[TESTER NAME]** [organisation, address] ("Tester"), dated [DATE].

1. **Purpose.** AfroBIM gives the Tester access to pre-release versions of AfroBIM, with its documents and related information ("Confidential Information"), solely so the Tester can test it and give feedback.
2. **Confidential Information** means the pre-release software, its features, screenshots, performance, defects, roadmap, pricing, and any non-public information AfroBIM shares, in any form.
3. **Duties.** The Tester will: keep it confidential and use it only for testing; not share it, screenshots or videos with anyone outside [the Tester's organisation, on a need-to-know basis]; not copy, reverse engineer or decompile the software, except as the law allows; not post about it publicly; and tell AfroBIM promptly of any leak.
4. **Exceptions.** Information that is public through no fault of the Tester, was lawfully known to the Tester before, was independently developed, or must be disclosed by law (with prompt notice to AfroBIM where lawful) is not covered.
5. **Ownership and feedback.** AfroBIM keeps all rights in the software. The Tester's feedback may be used by AfroBIM without payment. The Tester keeps ownership of their own project files.
6. **No warranty.** The pre-release software is provided "as is", may lose data, and must not be used for live project deliverables without independent checks by a qualified engineer.
7. **Term.** This agreement lasts from the date above until [two] years after the pre-release period ends. The software must be uninstalled and deleted when AfroBIM asks.
8. **Remedies.** Breach may cause harm that damages cannot repair, so AfroBIM may seek an injunction as well as other remedies.
9. **Governing law.** The laws of Uganda; the courts of Uganda have jurisdiction.
