# AfroBIM trademarks

> **Advice only. No trademark application has been filed yet.** Draft for review by a Ugandan advocate or registered trademark agent.

## Ownership and use rules

"AfroBIM" (the name) and the AfroBIM logo are trademarks of AfroBIM. The logo is still a placeholder.

You may:

* use the name to refer truthfully to the software, for example "a tool that opens files made with AfroBIM", or in a review or news item;
* link to the official download page.

You may not, without our written permission:

* use the name or logo in the name of your own product, company, domain name, social media account or app;
* suggest that your product or service is made, approved or endorsed by AfroBIM;
* distribute modified versions of AfroBIM under the name or logo;
* change, distort or recolour the logo, or combine it with another mark.

Unofficial copies, if any exist, are not AfroBIM. Report misuse to [CONTACT EMAIL].

## Recommendation: register the marks

An unregistered mark gives only limited protection in Uganda. We recommend registering:

1. **In Uganda, with URSB** (the Uganda Registration Services Bureau), under the Trademarks Act, 2010. File the word mark "AfroBIM" and, once final, the logo. Likely classes: **9** (downloadable software), **42** (software as a service, software design) and possibly **41** (training). Search the register first for conflicts.
2. **In other African markets** through **ARIPO** (the African Regional Intellectual Property Organization) under the Banjul Protocol, designating the member states where AfroBIM will sell. Uganda is a member of ARIPO; check which states are covered.
3. **Internationally** through the **Madrid System** (WIPO), which needs a base registration or application in a Madrid member country. Check Uganda's current status in the Madrid system and use ARIPO or another route if needed.

Also: register matching domain names and social handles, and use the (TM) symbol now and the (R) symbol only after registration.

This section is general information, not legal advice. Fees, classes and Uganda's position under ARIPO and Madrid must be confirmed with URSB or an advocate.
